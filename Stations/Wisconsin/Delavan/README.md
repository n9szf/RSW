# Delavan, Wis. passenger depot

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 76, drawing B-950, Oct 10 1911. Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 55 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
136 ft: baggage 24 x 25, 24 ft open pavilion, depot 88 x 25 (men's waiting 24 ft 6 x 25, ticket office 16 ft 6 x 13 ft 11, general waiting 34 ft 7 x 25); asbestos shingle roof, lap siding, centre gable over the office.
