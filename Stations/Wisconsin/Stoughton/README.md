# Stoughton, Wis. passenger station

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 106, built 1913 (drawn Apr 11 1919). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 17 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
129 ft 5 in incl. 27 ft 5 in pavilion; 27 ft 6 in wide; general waiting 26 x 36 ft 6, ticket office 18 ft 6 x 17, smoking 20 x 26, baggage 23 ft 6 x 26; cement plaster, brick base, shingle-tile roof, composition-roof pavilion.
