# Stoughton, Wis. passenger station (Milwaukee Road, 1913)

**HAND-BUILT 26 Nov 2026 - do not let the album generator overwrite Stoughton_Depot.scad.**

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot at 532 E. Main St., Stoughton. Built 1913, remodelled 1923 and 1945, closed in the 1970s, restored 1990, sold by the city to the Smet Foundation in 2025. Tracks on the east side, pavilion at the south (Main St.) end.
Model era: **1913 as built**.

Source drawing: Album of Depot Plans 1919, sheet 106 (built 1913, drawn Apr 11 1919). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots.

## From the drawing
129 ft 5 in incl. 27 ft 5 in pavilion; 27 ft 6 in wide; general waiting 26 x 36 ft 6, ticket office 18 ft 6 x 17, smoking 20 x 26, baggage 23 ft 6 x 26; cement plaster, brick base and corners, shingle-tile roof, composition-roof pavilion. Heights scaled off the sheet: plate 13.5 ft, ridge 24.5 ft, cross gable 22 ft.
