# Nashotah, Wis. passenger station

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 94, built 1915. Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 40 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
50 x 24 ft, waiting room 22 ft 6 x 40, ticket office 9 x 16 ft 6; stucco, asbestos shingles; built on a slope with a lower storey and grand stair on the track side (lower storey NOT modelled yet).
