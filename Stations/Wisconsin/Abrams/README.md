# Abrams, Wis. passenger station (built 1914)

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot on the old Milwaukee & Northern line, Green Bay to the Menominee River (milepost 134), Oconto County. Folder made 26 Nov 2026.
**HAND-BUILT** - not in the generator; do not let make_depots.py overwrite it.

Source drawing: Album of Depot Plans 1919, sheet 33 ("Passenger Station, Abrams, Wis., built in 1914"). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## History
The first depot (1881) stood on land owned by W. J. Abrams, the town's namesake. In 1914 the Abrams Business Men's Association complained to the Railroad Commission of Wisconsin that trains 2 and 3 did not stop properly and that the depot was too small for passengers and freight (decided 28 Jul 1914). The railroad started stopping the trains and submitted plans for a new depot - this building.

## Still standing?
Not known, probably gone. A web check on 26 Nov 2026 found no sign it survives: no Wisconsin Historical Society record, and the Milwaukee & Northern Historical Society's page of surviving line features shows only Abrams Feed & Supply at Main St. (E&LS trains, 2021 and 2024), no depot. Small M&N depots were often moved off as sheds or houses - worth asking in town (feed mill, town hall) on a road trip.

## From the drawing
52 ft 6 in x 24 ft: waiting room and office at the west end (office with a square bay on the track side), small warm room, freight room about 33 x 24 with freight doors opposite each other on both sides. Waiting-room door in the west end with a window each side. Gable roof, shingles; lap siding with belt and water table; vertical boards in the gables over a boxed cornice; brick chimney by the office.
