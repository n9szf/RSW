# Fond du Lac, Wis. passenger depot (1898)

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 132, Nov 1898 (J. U. Nettenstrom). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 65 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
24 x 60 ft; women's waiting 16 ft 6 x 22, office 12 x 15 with bay, men's waiting 17 x 22, baggage 11 x 22; brick veneer, stone trim, flared hip roof with hipped dormer, cresting.
