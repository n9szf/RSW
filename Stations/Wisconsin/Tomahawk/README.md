# Tomahawk, Wis. passenger station

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 26 Nov 2026. **Hand-built** (not written by the generator - do not overwrite).
Source drawing: Album sheet 100, built 1915 (drawn Chicago Aug 7 1916). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## Still stands
Yes. West end of Wisconsin Ave past Railway St (422 W Wisconsin Ave), Tomahawk, Lincoln County. Since 2006 a stove shop (Pellet Stove Junction, now Tomahawk Stove Junction). Agency closed about 1975. State survey 1982 (AHI 21954): Craftsman, stucco, wide bracketed eaves with exposed rafter ends, large pergola on Doric columns. The shop says it repainted the outside to stand out, so today's colours are probably not railroad colours.

## From the drawing
Building 121 ft 11 in x 26 ft: baggage and freight room, warm room and coal room (82 ft 1-1/2 in), ticket office (13 ft 5-1/2 in front), smoking room, general waiting room (26 ft 4 in). Stucco, slate roof, gable ends, cross gable over the trackside ticket-office front. 5 ft 6 in gap, then an open shelter 41 ft (2 x 20 ft 6 in) x 26 ft on 12 columns in pairs. Plank freight platform and incline on the town side at the north end.
