# Watertown, Wis. passenger depot (Frost & Granger)

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 48, drawing 6945, Sept 1896 (Frost & Granger). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 40 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
168 ft overall: depot 88 x 30 ft (women's and men's waiting rooms 36 ft 6, office with bay), 40 ft pavilion, 40 x 30 baggage room; stone base, brick walls, hip roofs, centre gable dormer with lantern.
