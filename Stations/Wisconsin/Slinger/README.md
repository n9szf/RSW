# Schleisingerville (Slinger), Wis. passenger depot

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 74, drawing B-1026, Oct 30 1911. Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 65 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
70 ft 6 in x 24 ft + 32 ft pavilion; freight and baggage 24 ft 3 x 23 ft 6, smoking 11 ft 11 x 23, office 12 ft 6 x 17, general waiting 19 ft 4 x 23 ft 6; hipped shingle roof, lap siding.
