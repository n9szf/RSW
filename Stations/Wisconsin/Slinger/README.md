# Schleisingerville (Slinger), Wis. passenger depot (1911)

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
**HAND-BUILT 27 Nov 2026 - do not overwrite with the album generator.**
Source drawing: Album sheet 74, drawing B-1026, Chicago Oct 30 1911 (track side and end elevations, floor plan, half section). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 65 miles from Madison.

## The building now
It still stands. It was moved from the MILW/Soo crossing and turned about 90 degrees to face the road at 414 Kettle Moraine Dr S. It was restored in 1986 as the E.H. Wolf & Sons office, and since 2020 it has been the Schleisingerville to Slinger Historical Museum (free; open some Sundays, Wednesday evenings and the 4th Saturday - check before going). The museum has historic photos: the old and new depots together in 1912, the depot in 1954, and Soo Line trains passing.
The earlier depot (gable roof, board and batten) was moved to 121 Storck St as a house. The Schleisingerville postcard in reference / Station photos web / Slinger (08 and 14) shows that older depot, not this one.

## From the drawing
- 70 ft 6 in x 24 ft, plus an open pavilion 32 ft long at the waiting-room end.
- Track side: 38 ft 2 to the office bay, bay 12 ft, then 20 ft 4. Town side: 37 ft 2 to the toilet annex, annex 13 ft 6, then 19 ft 10.
- Rooms: freight and baggage 24-3 x 23-0, smoking 11-11 x 23-0, office 12-6 x 17-0, general waiting 19-4 x 23-6.
- Floor to ceiling 12 ft 2. 4 in lap siding above the belt, V-ceiling set vertical below it. Shingle hip roof. Boxed cornice with frieze and pilasters; no knee brackets drawn on the main building.
- Pavilion: hip roof, lower and narrower than the main roof; twin tapered columns on square pedestals.
