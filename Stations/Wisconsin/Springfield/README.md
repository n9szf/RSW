# Springfield, Wis. passenger depot

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 82, drawing B-943, May 29 1912. Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 60 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
80 x 24 ft; waiting 18 ft 3, office 12 ft 6 with bay, smoking 12 ft 6, freight room 36 ft 9; hipped shingle roof, lap siding.
