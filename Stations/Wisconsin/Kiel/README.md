# Kiel, Wis. passenger station

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 93, built 1916 (drawn Aug 9 1916). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 95 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
113 ft 8 in + 28 ft pavilion; station 53 ft 8 x 26 (general waiting 19 ft 6 x 24 ft 8, ticket office 12 ft 2 x 15 ft 2, smoking 11 ft 6 x 24 ft 8), freight and baggage 62 x 24 (tar and gravel); stucco, asbestos shingles, clerestory dormer.
