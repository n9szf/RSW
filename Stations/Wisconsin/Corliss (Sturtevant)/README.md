# Corliss (Sturtevant), Wis. junction depot

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 39, drawing A-725, May 16 1901 (J. U. Nettenstrom). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 70 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
68 x 68 ft L at the junction of two lines: smoking 15 x 19, general waiting 27 x 27 with octagonal office bay 15 x 15, two 24 and 20 ft pavilions, baggage 15 x 19; tall conical turret over the office, cresting.
