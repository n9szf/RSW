# Horicon, Wis. passenger station

**HAND-BUILT (26 Nov 2026) - the album generator must not overwrite OpenSCAD/Horicon_Depot.scad.**

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot, 511 Barstow St, Horicon. Still standing: former MILW depot, now a Wisconsin & Southern office, inside the wye (2015 photos; 2018 Street View).
Source drawing: Album sheet 86, built 1914 (drawn Apr 10 1917). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the drawing
- 110 ft overall: building 86 ft 4 x 18 ft + open pavilion 24 ft at the east end (two posts at its outer end).
- Waiting-room wing on the town side: 18 ft wide, projects 14 ft, at 26 ft 6 - 44 ft 6 from the west end, hipped roof into the main ridge.
- Freight room at the west end; general waiting room, ticket office, smoking room, toilets.
- "Old building 42 ft" = the east part (about 44 - 86 ft); the west part with the wing is the 1914 enlargement.
- One continuous shingle hip roof; lap siding over a vertical-board wainscot; two chimneys on the ridge; eave brackets.
