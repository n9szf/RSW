# Viroqua, Wis. - Milwaukee Road passenger depot (1899)

**Hand-built - do not regenerate.** Rev 2, 26 Nov 2026 (rev 2 adds the 1899 interior).

- Stands: 305 Railroad Ave, Viroqua, on its original site. Wonderstate Coffee (formerly Kickapoo Coffee) uses it for offices; it was their roastery for about ten years. Earlier: Howard Johnson's Enterprises (fertilizer). Sparta - Viroqua branch, torn up 1982.
- Plan: Album of Depot Plans 1919, Sheet 130, "24' x 64' Depot as built at Viroqua", Nov 12 1899, J.U. Nettenstrom architect (private reference).

## Files
- `OpenSCAD/Viroqua_Depot.scad` - the model. SCALE 29 G / 48 O / 87 HO. Uses `../../../_common/rsw_depot.scad` for walls, windows, doors and colours; roof, turret, bay and brackets are drawn in the file.
- `Notes/` - research notes and preview pictures.
- Web photos: `RSW/reference/Station photos web/Viroqua/` (index.csv gives each source).
- 3D file: `Blender/GLB/stations/Viroqua_Depot.glb` (run `Processed/Make Viroqua GLB.bat`).

## Shape and colours
- Shape kept to the album plan (Tom's rule): turret over the bay and twin gable windows are modelled, although the turret seems gone and the gable windows were sided over by 2014.
- Colours about 2014 (Tom 26 Nov 2026): golden yellow siding, deep red trim and doors, black roof, buff chimneys. Other eras seen in photos: 1976 dark brown with a lighter brown gable; Sept 2025 slate blue-gray with dark blue trim.

## Interior (rev 2)
Rooms from the sheet plan: baggage room with the toilet room in its town-side corner (door from the women's room), women's waiting room, agent's office with the bay and a ticket window into each waiting room, men's waiting room (door to the office). Furniture and finishes are ESTIMATE: oak wainscot, buff plaster, varnished beadboard ceiling at 9.5 ft, pine floor, wall benches, a round iron stove in each waiting room, operator's table in the bay, ticket counters, desk, safe, baggage scale, two-wheel truck, trunks. `-D SHOW_ROOF=false` gives a cut-away view.

## Known (sheet)
24 x 64 ft; rooms in a row - baggage 16'6", women's 16'6", office 12' with the bay, men's 16'6" (men's at the x = 64 end; seen from the track it is on the left, as on the sheet). Track-side openings, three-sided bay, steep hip roof with gables on both ends (twin windows), ridge cresting, octagonal turret with a tall spire over the bay, two chimneys on the ridge, long eave brackets, lap siding.

## ESTIMATE (fix on a road trip)
Heights measured off the sheet (wall 12.2 ft to the soffit, eaves 7.5 ft, ridge about 26 ft, spire about 33 ft). Town-side openings (not drawn on the sheet), the baggage end, bracket spacing, cresting pattern, turret size in plan, the platform, name boards, sash and chimney colours.
