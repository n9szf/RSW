# Racine Junction, Wis. union depot (C.M.&St.P. and C.&N.W.)

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheets 141-142, drawing 9804, Apr 6 1900 (J. U. Nettenstrom). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 70 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
63 ft 10 in x 24 ft (35 ft 9 at the octagon); octagonal centre with back-to-back C.&N.W. and C.M.&St.P. waiting rooms 17 x 22, two offices 10 x 15, baggage 15 x 22; stone base, bell-cast hip roofs, cresting. Octagon shown as a square block for now.
