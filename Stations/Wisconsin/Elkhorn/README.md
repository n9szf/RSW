# Elkhorn, Wis. passenger station

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 96, built 1914 (drawn Mar 11 1916); unbuilt 1913 design on sheet 127. Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 55 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
110 x 20 ft + 30 ft pavilion (tar and gravel roof); baggage 21 x 24 ft 6, smoking 22 ft 6 x 24 ft 4, ticket office 15 ft 5 x 17 ft 2, general waiting 26 ft 4 x 36 ft 8; stucco, slate roof, centre gable.
