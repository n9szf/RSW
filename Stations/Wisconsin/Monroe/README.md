# Monroe, Wis. passenger depot (J. T. W. Jennings)

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 144 (marked 14), drawing 2005, July 1888 (J. T. W. Jennings). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 45 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
62 ft 6 in x 20 ft; two waiting rooms 24 ft 3, octagonal office bay 11 ft, baggage 12 ft; steep hipped roof with flared eaves, gabled dormer, iron cresting; lap siding with vertical boards below.
