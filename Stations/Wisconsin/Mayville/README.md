# Mayville, Wis. passenger and freight depot

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 140, drawing 7418, July 25 1897 (J. U. Nettenstrom). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 55 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
24 x 100 ft passenger and freight depot; freight room, smoking 9 x 20, office 14 x 20 with bay, waiting room 23 x 24; board-and-batten gables, lap siding.
