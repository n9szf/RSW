# Milton Junction, Wis. depot (1923)

Status: research only, no model yet (25 Nov 2026). HAND-BUILT when made - do not let the generator overwrite.

- Where: 231 Front St., Milton (old village of Milton Junction). Stands, much altered: stucco over the brick, was Klig's Union Depot restaurant, caboose attached on the west side, fire 2012.
- Built 1923 for the C&NW (City of Milton 2013 survey). Also used by the Milwaukee Road (Milton Junction, MP 63.7). Probably a union depot - to confirm.
- Replaced the Morgan House hotel, which had been the depot since 1861.
- As built: one storey, rectangular, brick, jerkinhead (clipped-gable) roof, wide eaves. Everything else unknown.
- Model: 1923 as built, no stucco, no caboose. Hand-build on _common\rsw_depot.scad and add to CUSTOM in make_depots.py. All sizes ESTIMATE until a Sanborn sheet or plan turns up.
- Colours: none known. Default brick red body, dark gray trim.
