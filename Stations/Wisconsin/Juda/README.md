# Juda, Wis. passenger station

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 124, drawing B-?, June 1 1928. Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 40 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
44 x 24 ft depot + 24 ft exterior high platform; baggage and freight room, waiting room, ticket office with bay; drop siding, gable roof.
