# West Salem, Wis. passenger station

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) brick combination depot, 1903, on the La Crosse line. Stood on the north side of the tracks, east of Leonard St. (chicagorailfan.com "Lost Stations"). **Gone** - no sign that it survives (26 Nov 2026 search). The Chicago & North Western depot stood across the tracks from it.

## Model (first shape)
- 131'8" x 24'. West to east: women's waiting 21'6", office 16' with a canted bay on the track side, men's waiting 19'10", freight room 74'4" (scale). Seat alcove on the town side of the passage.
- Passenger end: steep hip roof with finials and ridge cap, bell-cast kick over 7 ft eaves, bracketed frieze, stone base and banded stone corners. Freight end: lower hip roof, 6 ft eaves, two paneled double freight doors each side, door in the east end.
- Three chimneys (west end wall, office, men's room / freight wall), iron stay on the west chimney.
- Data run west to east as on the sheet; the model is mirrored so that, seen from the track, the passenger end is on the left (as drawn).
- Colours ESTIMATE (no colour photo; Tom asked for brick colour): brick #8E4A36, stone #A99B80, dark gray trim and sash #55585A, stained shingles #4A3F3A.
- ESTIMATE: every height (floor 1.5, frieze top about 11.5, eave edge 11.2, ridges 26.7 and 21.5 ft - scaled off the elevation), town side and east end openings, window and door sizes, bracket spacing, quoin pattern, platform. No interior yet.
