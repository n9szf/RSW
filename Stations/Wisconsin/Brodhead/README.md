# Brodhead, Wis. - Milwaukee Road freight and passenger depot (1881)

**Hand-built - do not regenerate.** Rev 2, 26 Nov 2026.

- Stands: 1108 1st Center Ave, Brodhead - Brodhead Historical Society Depot Museum (restored 1980, opened May 1981). Mineral Point Division (Janesville - Brodhead - Monroe), now the WSOR. Main track on the north side. FM H10-44 #781 and a caboose are on the old house track south of the depot.
- Date: Historical Society 1881 (rebuilt after a July 1881 fire); NRHP district nomination 84000724 says c.1885. It is on the April 1887 Sanborn map either way.
- No album sheet or MPL drawing exists for it. The model is built from the Sanborn maps, the NRHP text, a c.1914 photo and Street View (see Notes/Brodhead research.md).

## Known (from the sources)
30 x 103 ft, 1 storey, brick veneer on a frame, 16 ft walls, shingle gable roof with wide eaves on big scrolled brackets, stilted segmental hoods over every opening, triple round-arched window in the west gable with the BRODHEAD board below it, one window in the east gable, three-sided bay on the track side about 31 ft from the west end (1887-1915; gone today), frame addition about 29 x 30 ft on the east end (built 1900-1915) on a 3 ft freight-platform floor. Track-side windows and doors measured from Street View.

## ESTIMATE (fix on a road trip)
Roof pitch (7 in 12 from the c.1914 photo), 5 ft eaves, town-side openings (hidden behind loco 781), freight doors on the track side before the museum, room layout, 1887 trim / roof / door colours (Milwaukee Road defaults), the addition's roof and 1915 doors. The 2017-18 Knight addition (L-shaped, south-east) is not modelled.
