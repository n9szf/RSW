# Hartland, Wis. passenger station

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 111, built 1917 (drawn Aug 26 1919). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 45 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
63 ft 4 in station + pavilions (15 ft 4 in and 60 ft, tar and gravel roofs); baggage 19 x 27, smoking 16 ft 6 x 27, ticket office 15 ft 8 x 17 ft 10, general waiting 24 ft 10 x 37 ft 4; stucco, red asphalt shingles, half-timbered gable.
