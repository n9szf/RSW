# Woodman, Wis. passenger station (built 1914)

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot on the Prairie du Chien line. Folder made 26 Nov 2026.
**HAND-BUILT** - not in the generator; do not let make_depots.py overwrite it.

Source drawing: Album of Depot Plans 1919, sheet 105 (drawn Chicago May 20 1919). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## Still standing?
Not known, probably gone. A web check on 26 Nov 2026 found no sign it survives (no Wisconsin Historical Society record, no later photos). The only photo lead is woodmanwi.net (village site), "Woodman Depot 1914", credited to Doyle Lewis and the Unique Cafe, Boscobel: https://woodmanwi.net/woodmandepot1914.html - not downloaded.

## From the drawing
52 ft 2 in x 24 ft 2 in: waiting room 14 x 23, smoking room 11 ft 4 x 13, ticket office 11 ft 4 x 13 with a square 9 ft bay on the track side, baggage and freight 23 x 23 with a 4-ton scale. Gable roof, shingles, quarter pitch. 6 in drop siding, cement curbing, eave brackets. One high window in each gable end.
