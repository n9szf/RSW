# Stowell, Wis. passenger depot

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 69, drawing B-586, Sept 14 1909. Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
Location to confirm.

## From the drawing
56 x 20 ft; general waiting 16 x 19, office 11 ft 6 x 9 with bay, smoking 10 x 19, baggage and express 16 x 19; flared hipped shingle roof, diamond-pane upper sash, lap siding.
