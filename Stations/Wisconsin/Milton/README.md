# Milton, Wis. passenger station

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 99, built 1914. Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 33 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
101 ft 6 in: freight and baggage 49 ft 6 x 18 (tar and gravel roof), station 52 x 28 (shingle gable roof); waiting 24 x 25, ticket office 14 x 14, smoking 12 ft 6 x 25 ft 6; brick; pavilion at the waiting-room end.
