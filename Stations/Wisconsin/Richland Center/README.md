# Richland Center, Wis. passenger depot

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 67, drawing B-616, Nov 3 1909. Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 55 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
64 x 20 ft; baggage 12 ft 6 x 19, smoking 16 x 19, ticket office 11 ft 10 x 9 with bay, women's waiting 16 x 19; hipped shingle roof with flared eaves, diamond-pane upper sash.
