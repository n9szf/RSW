# Edgerton, Wis. passenger depot

Milwaukee Road (C.M.&St.P.) depot. Folder made 24 Nov 2026.
Source drawing: Album sheet 58, drawing A-7532, June 19 1906 (J. A. Lindstrand). Private reference (in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.
About 25 miles from Madison.

## From the drawing
128 ft overall: building 98 x 24 ft + 30 ft pavilion; baggage 24, smoking 21 ft 10 x 20, office with bay, general waiting 36 ft 4 x 21 ft 10; brick veneer below, stained shingle roof, half pitch.
