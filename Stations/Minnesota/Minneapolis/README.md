# Minneapolis, Minnesota - Milwaukee Road Depot (1899)

**HAND-BUILT model - do not let the depot generator overwrite it.**

Status 26 Nov 2026: rev 1 first shape in `OpenSCAD/Minneapolis_Depot.scad` (TDQ-219, TDQ-646).
Tom 11/26: model all of it - head house with the 1899 tower, the midway, the 625 ft train shed and the baggage building - in G, O and HO (set SCALE = 29 / 48 / 87 at the top of the file).

The 1899 Charles S. Frost depot: pink granite first story, buff ("yellow") brick above, Roman arches through stories 2-3, a 140 ft tower with a Giralda-style spire (removed May 1941), a 625 x 100 ft train shed on one 100 ft Fink truss span over five stub tracks, and a 225 x 20 ft brick baggage building with a 75 ft chimney. The building still stands (Renaissance Minneapolis Hotel, The Depot).

Switches at the top of the .scad: SHOW_SHED, SHOW_BAGGAGE, SHOW_TRACKS, SHOW_UMBRELLA (off).

Size: the complex is about 760 ft long - G about 26 ft (8 m), O about 16 ft, HO about 9 ft. Not cut into print sections yet.
