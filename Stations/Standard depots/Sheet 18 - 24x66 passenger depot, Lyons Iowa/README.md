# Standard 24 x 66 ft passenger depot, Lyons, Iowa (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1898)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 18.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
24 x 66 ft; men's waiting room 20, office 12 with bay, women's waiting room 19, baggage room 12 ft 6; steep flared hipped shingle roof with iron cresting, lap siding with vertical boards below.
