# Standard 24 x 95 ft depot, Coon Rapids (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1898)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 19.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
24 x 95 ft; two waiting rooms 17, office 11 with bay, baggage room 10, freight room 48 (low and high floors) with a high platform; hipped shingle roof with cresting, lap siding over drop siding.
