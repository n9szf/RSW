# Standard 20 x 50 ft passenger depot, Libertyville, Ill. (C.M.&St.P., Jan 15 1900)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 09.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
20 x 50 ft; two waiting rooms 12 ft 6, octagonal office bay 11 ft, baggage room 11 ft 6; steep flared hipped roof with cresting, lap siding.
