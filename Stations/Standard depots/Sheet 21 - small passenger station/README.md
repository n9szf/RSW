# Small passenger station, Webster, Minn. (C.M.&St.P. Engineering Dept., built 1916)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 21.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
26 x 18 ft; office 11 ft 6 with bay, waiting room, freight room 11 ft 6; gable roof (asbestos shingle) with lean-to over the freight end, 6 in drop siding.
