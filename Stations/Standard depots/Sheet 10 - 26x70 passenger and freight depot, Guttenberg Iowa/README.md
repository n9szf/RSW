# Standard 26 x 70 ft passenger and freight depot, Guttenberg, Iowa (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1898)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 10.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
26 x 70 ft; two waiting rooms 17 ft 4, office 11 with bay, freight room 39 ft (low and high floors) with a high platform; hipped shingle roof with cresting, lap siding over drop siding.
