# Standard 16 x 26 ft depot, East Moline (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1898)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 02.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
16 x 26 ft; waiting room 14, office 11 with bay; shingle roof with cresting, drop siding.
