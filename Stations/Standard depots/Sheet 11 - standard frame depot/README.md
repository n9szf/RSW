# Standard frame passenger station 17 x 33 ft 4 in (C.M.&St.P. Engineering Dept., c.1916)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 11.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
17 x 33 ft 4 in; waiting room 10, office 9 with bay, baggage and freight room 14 ft 4; asbestos roofing, lap siding; used at Knoke, Iowa (1925), Bethesda, Wis., Dover, S.D..
