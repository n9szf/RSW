# Standard 24 x 100 ft passenger and freight depot, Elk Point, S.D. (C.M.&St.P., Nov 17 1899)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 22.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
24 x 100 ft: freight room 45 ft 6 with a high platform, waiting room 18 ft 6, office 15 with bay, waiting room 18 ft 6; flared hipped roofs, the passenger end higher, cresting, lap siding.
