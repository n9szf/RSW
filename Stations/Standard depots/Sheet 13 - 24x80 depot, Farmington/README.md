# Standard 24 x 90 ft depot, Farmington (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1898)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 13.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
24 x 90 ft (the sheet says 24 x 90): waiting room 14, office 14 with bay, waiting room 14, baggage room 12, freight room 35; flared hipped shingle roof with cresting, lap siding over drop siding.
