# Passenger station, Oakwood, Wis. (C.M.&St.P. Engineering Dept., built 1913)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 17.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
38 x 24 ft (38 ft 1 in); waiting room, office 9 ft 6 x 15 with bay, baggage and freight room 15 ft x 14 ft 8; hipped asphalt-shingle roof, drop siding with vertical boards below.
