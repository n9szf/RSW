# Standard 18 x 36 ft depot (C.M.&St.P., Jan 1900)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 03.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
18 x 36 ft; waiting room 18 ft 8, freight room 15 ft 6, office 10 ft with bay; shingle roof, drop siding; built at Whalan, Twin Brooks, James, Granada, Chandler, Bixby, Utica, St. Olaf, Alpha, Okabena, Vienna, Pratt, Trent, Fostoria, Long Grove, Buena Vista.
