# Standard 24 x 60 ft depot (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1899)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 08.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
24 x 60 ft; waiting room 17, office 11 with bay, freight room 30; hipped shingle roof with cresting, lap siding over drop siding; built at Elm Springs, Middleton, So. Amana, Dexter and Arlington.
