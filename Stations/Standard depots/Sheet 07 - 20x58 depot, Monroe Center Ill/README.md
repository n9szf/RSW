# Standard 20 x 58 ft depot, Monroe Center, Ill. (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1898)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 07.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
20 x 58 ft; waiting room 17 x 19, office 10 x 19 with bay, freight room 29 x 19; shingle roof, board-and-batten gables, drop siding below.
