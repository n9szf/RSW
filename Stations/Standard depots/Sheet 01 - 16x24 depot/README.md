# Standard 16 x 24 ft depot (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1898)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 01.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
16 x 24 ft; waiting room 11 x 8, office with bay, freight room 11 ft 4 x 16; shingle roof with cresting, drop siding; as built at Medary(ville?) and Riggs; also used at Arbor Vitae, Oconto Jct and Knowlton Wis., Templeton, Marvin, Bell Center and others.
