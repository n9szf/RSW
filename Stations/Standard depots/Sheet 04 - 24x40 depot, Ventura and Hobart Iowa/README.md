# Standard 24 x 40 ft depot, Ventura and Hobart, Iowa (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1898)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 04.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
24 x 40 ft; waiting room 14, freight room 26, office 16 with bay; shingle roof with cresting, drop siding; also at Bassett and Chatsworth, Iowa.
