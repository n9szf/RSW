# Standard 20 x 48 ft depot (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1898)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 05.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
20 x 48 ft; waiting room 19 ft 8, office 10 with bay, freight room 27 ft 6; shingle roof, drop siding; built at Summit, Morrisonville, Pierpont.
