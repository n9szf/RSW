# Standard 24 x 60 ft depot with living rooms (C.M.&St.P., Jan 16 1900) - sheets 14 and 15

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 14 (elevation; the plans are on sheet 15).
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
60 ft overall: two-storey part 28 ft 6 x 24 (waiting room and office with bay below; kitchen, sitting room and two bedrooms above) and a one-storey freight room 30 x 24; hipped roofs with cresting, cross gable over the bay, lap siding below, drop siding above; built at Rockland Mich., Lavinia, Lytton, Varina, Webb, Gillet Grove, Nemaha and Northam, Iowa.
