# Standard 20 x 96 ft depot with pavilions, Grays Lake (C.M.&St.P.)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 12.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
96 ft overall: pavilion 20 ft, depot 40 x 20 (two waiting rooms 13 ft 6, octagonal office bay 11 ft) with a centre gable, pavilion 20 ft, baggage room 16 ft; flared hipped shingle roofs with cresting.
