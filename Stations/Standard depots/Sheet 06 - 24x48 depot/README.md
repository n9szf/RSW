# Standard 24 x 48 ft depot (C.M.&St.P., Nov 1898)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 06.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
24 x 48 ft; waiting room 18 ft 6, office 14 with bay, freight room 28; shingle roof with cresting, drop siding; built at Grafton, Stiles, Walford and Nekoosa.
