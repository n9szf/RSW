# Passenger station, Glenview, Ill. (C.M.&St.P. Engineering Dept., c.1912)

Milwaukee Road standard depot plan from the 1919 Album of Depot Plans, sheet 16.
Drawing (private, in copyright): Research Library / RSW / Stations / Standard depots / Album of Depot Plans 1919.

## From the sheet
66 x 20 ft; smoking room 15 ft 8 x 18 ft 4, ticket office with bay, general waiting room 25 x 18 ft 4; shingle gable roof with centre dormer, lap siding, three chimneys.
