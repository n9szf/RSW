## Paint (1938, check TDQ-592)
Grey shroud and cab; black cowling; orange-yellow band with maroon edges on the skirting; tender maroon with orange-yellow bands; wheels grey with maroon axle rings; polished rods.

## Rev B (22 Nov)
Shroud rebuilt as boiler casing + skirt ledge + black cowling (ALCO sections and photos); rounded bullet nose with stainless wing bands, headlight, grille, marker lamps; arched cab with framed windows, mullions, grab irons and steps; Boxpok drivers with box ribs and counterweights; trucks with journal boxes and cast side frames; Delta trailing truck; Walschaert valve gear; boiler handrails; tender with rounded top and rear, coal bunker hood, water hatch, top rails, rear ladders, footboard; "THE MILWAUKEE ROAD" and "100" lettering in the preview (decals on printed kits).

## Rev C (22 Nov)
Bell (under the nose behind the pilot - position a best guess, TDQ-598), three-chime whistle and air horn on the smokebox front with the horn mouth showing through an opening in the cowling front (1938 article and ALCO elevation), sand pipes, clasp brake shoes and hangers, driver brake cylinders, Pyle-National generator behind No. 3 driver, front-end throttle rod, injector checks and blower pipes, lubricators on the running board, hinged air-pump access panels, builder's and Kuhler name plates, cylinder cocks, speed recorder drive, pilot air hoses, cab roof ventilator and visors, tender back-up light, rear marker lamps, front steps and grab irons, brake cylinders and air reservoir. Skirt widened round the cylinders.

## Rev D and E (22 Nov)
Rev D: cab to the ALCO cab section (straight sides to 12'-8", tight corners, taller windows); firebox casing brought down into the cab front.
Rev E: number boards - streamlined housings on the casing shoulder either side of the cowling (builder photo), "100" on the outer face (decal on printed kits), forward marker lens; stainless nose bands raised to match the photo.
